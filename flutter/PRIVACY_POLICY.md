# Privacy Policy for Job Search Mobile

**Last Updated:** February 16, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Job Search Mobile ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Account Information:** Email address, name, phone number
- **Profile Information:** Resume/CV, profile picture, work experience, education
- **Application Data:** Job applications submitted through the App
- **Communications:** Messages, feedback, and support requests

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features used, time spent in the App
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Log Data:** IP address, access times, crashes, and errors
- **Location Data:** Approximate location based on IP address (if enabled)

### Information from Third Parties

- **Firebase Authentication:** We use Firebase for authentication which may collect device and usage data
- **Job Listings:** Information from job posting services and employer partners

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide and maintain the App's functionality
- Create and manage your account
- Match you with relevant job opportunities
- Send notifications about job applications and updates
- Improve and personalize your experience
- Respond to your requests and support needs
- Ensure security and prevent fraud
- Comply with legal obligations

## Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with:

- **Employers:** When you apply for jobs, we share your application materials
- **Service Providers:** Third-party services that help us operate the App (Firebase, cloud hosting)
- **Analytics Partners:** To understand app usage and improve our services
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law or to protect our rights

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in
- Regular security audits and updates
- Access controls and monitoring

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction:** Update or correct inaccurate information
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your account and data
- **Opt-Out:** Unsubscribe from promotional emails
- **Data Portability:** Export your data in a standard format

To exercise these rights, contact us at whdgurtler@gmail.com.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:

- Your account is active
- Needed to provide services
- Required by law or legitimate business purposes

After account deletion, we may retain certain information for legal compliance.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.
