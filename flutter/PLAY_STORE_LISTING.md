## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Job Search helps you discover and apply to job opportunities that match your skills and experience. Upload your resume, set up automated job scraping, and browse curated job listings — all from your phone.

**Key Features:**

- Browse and search job listings with powerful filters (location, employment type, experience level)
- Upload your resume for personalized job matching
- Set up automated job scraping to find new opportunities daily
- Track scrape progress in real-time
- Apply to jobs directly from the app
- Secure authentication with Firebase

**How It Works:**

1. Create an account and upload your resume
2. Set up scrape configs with your target companies and keywords
3. Trigger scrapes to find matching jobs
4. Browse results and apply to positions that interest you

**Smart Job Matching:**
Our system analyzes job postings against your resume to surface the most relevant opportunities, saving you hours of manual searching.

**Privacy First:**
Your data is encrypted and never sold to third parties. You control what information is shared with employers.

Built for job seekers who want a smarter, more efficient way to find their next role.
